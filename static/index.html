HOW TO PLAY

UNO Rules

01 — MATCHPlay a card matching the current card's color or value.
02 — DRAWPress DRAW to take one card and end your turn.
03 — WILDWILD lets you choose Red, Blue, Green or Yellow.
04 — +1+1 adds one card to the penalty. +1 cards can be stacked.
05 — +4+4 adds four cards and lets you choose a color. +4 can be stacked.
06 — REVERSEReverses the direction of play.
07 — SKIPSkips the next player.
08 — WINEmpty your hand first to win.

House rule: playing an invalid card automatically draws one card and ends your turn.
